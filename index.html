The curator

About Roos

I'm Roos, and I have always been drawn to things that still carry the mark of the hands that made them: a bangle shaped over a flame in a family workshop, a length of cotton printed one wooden block at a time, a steel tiffin made to be carried to work every day for decades. Objects like these hold the story of their making, and you can feel it the moment one is in your hands.

Gulab grew out of my time in India, wandering through markets, back lanes and small workshops where makers still work the way their parents and grandparents did. Wherever I can, I buy directly from the people who make the pieces, so I know where each one comes from and who shaped it. Much of what I bring back is handmade or made in small batches, which means quantities are always limited and no two arrivals are quite the same.

Some pieces are for celebrations, some for everyday, and some, like a good tiffin, are simply made to last a lifetime. I choose every one as if I were choosing it for myself, pack it with care, and send it on to its new home anywhere in Europe.

— Roos
